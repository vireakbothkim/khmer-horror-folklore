title: Ahp(Krasue)
khmer name: អាប
the story: Ahp(Krasue) is a bodyless woman flying entity with all the human organs attached to the head. The entity appears to have sharp eyes, glowing, flickering red light from the heart, and long hair flying with the late night wind. In urban legends, Ahp seeks blood like a vampire. They desire farm animal organs, especially chickens, blood of newly delivered human newborn, and any types of organs.
